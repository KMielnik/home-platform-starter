# Contributing

Contributions should make the adaptive method clearer, safer, or easier to
verify for a new technical user.

- Keep examples generic and non-deploying unless a file explicitly says
  otherwise.
- Never submit private addresses, identifiers, credentials, raw backups, or
  household-specific topology.
- Explain why a new tool or layer earns its operational cost.
- Prefer a small checkpoint, a testable outcome, and a recovery path.
- Put durable concepts in `docs/`, reusable forms in `templates/`, and safe
  examples in `examples/`.
- Mark claims as verified, documented, proposed, or unknown.
- Run `python scripts/validate_repo.py` before opening a change.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is provided under the MIT
License in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
